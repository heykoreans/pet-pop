❓ How to Play

🎮 Basics

Tap two adjacent pets to swap them. Match 3 or more in a row or column to pop them and score points.

When pets pop, others fall down to fill the gaps — chain matches together for big combo bonuses!

⭐ Special Blocks

Bigger matches create powerful blocks!

⏰ Clock Pet

After 20 seconds, golden clock pets start dropping onto the board. Match 3 clocks in a row to gain +3 seconds per clock cleared.

Clocks tend to land near each other — keep an eye out and snag them before time runs low!

🔥 Combos & FEVER

Each cascading match builds your combo count. The higher the combo, the bigger the score multiplier.

Hit 5+ combo in a single chain to trigger FEVER mode with extra effects!

✨ Last Pop Bonus

Any match made in the final 2 seconds earns a 2× score bonus. Save your biggest plays for the end!

🔄 Auto Shuffle

If no matches are possible, the board automatically reshuffles itself and you get a bonus +5 seconds. Stuck boards become opportunities!

🌟 Daily Challenge

Same Board · One Shot · Pure Skill

Every day at midnight ET, a new puzzle appears — and everyone gets the exact same board. No luck, no random advantage. Just you vs. every other player on the same starting position.

  • One attempt per day per player
  • 🔥 Streak counts how many days in a row you've played — wear it on the leaderboard
  • Miss a day and your streak resets to 0 — don't break the chain!
  • The Today tab on the leaderboard shows only today's daily plays

📚 Pet Dex

Unlock new pets by:

  • Reaching cumulative score milestones (5k, 12k, 25k, ...)
  • Completing missions (max combo, last pop streaks, etc.)

Pets are currently avatar-style cosmetics — tap one in the Dex to equip it on your profile. Powers coming in a future update!
